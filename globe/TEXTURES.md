# Textures

`build_earth.py` loads three equirectangular maps from its own directory and resizes each to 2048×1024. They are
ordinary JPEGs; the `.bin` name only keeps them out of image indexers. All three are included here.

| file | content | source | licence |
|---|---|---|---|
| `earth_clean.bin` | daytime surface, no clouds | NASA Visible Earth, Blue Marble `land_shallow_topo_2048.jpg` | public domain (NASA) |
| `earth_night.bin` | city lights on black | NASA Earth Observatory, *The Earth at Night*, via Wikimedia Commons `The_earth_at_night.jpg` | public domain (NASA) |
| `clouds.bin` | cloud cover, white on black | [Solar System Scope](https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/) `2k_earth_clouds.jpg` | [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), © Solar System Scope |
